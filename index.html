Behind the drizzle

Made by hand.
Made with heart.

Dip and Drizzle Treat Co. started with a simple love for creating sweet treats and making life’s special moments a little sweeter.

As a full-time working professional, wife, and mom of two active children, life is always busy. Between my career, family, sports, and everyday responsibilities, I somehow found another passion—creating beautiful, handcrafted treats that bring smiles to people’s faces.

What began as something I simply enjoyed doing quickly grew into Dip and Drizzle Treat Co., a small business built from creativity, hard work, and a love for celebrating others.

Each of our treats is handcrafted with care and attention to detail. We specialize in cakesicles, cake pops, chocolate-covered strawberries, Oreos, pretzels, Rice Krispie treats, and custom creations designed especially for your celebration.

Balancing a full-time career, motherhood, family, and a small business isn't always easy, but Dip and Drizzle is truly a labor of love. There is something incredibly rewarding about taking an idea and turning it into a beautiful—and delicious—part of someone's special day.

From birthdays and weddings to baby showers, sporting events, holidays, corporate events, and everything in between, we love creating treats that are just as special as the moments you're celebrating.

At Dip and Drizzle Treat Co., it's about more than chocolate and sprinkles. It's about creativity, family, celebrations, and making memories—one sweet treat at a time.
